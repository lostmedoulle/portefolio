01

Financial Reconciliation & Audit Platform

Weeks of manual reconciliation across the pension ERP, the accounting system and ledgers.

~1.8Mtransactions reconciled
20+pension foundations
3–4 wksaved per foundation (est.)
ExtractPlaywright→Normalizedatasets→Reconcileexact · temporal · invariants→Classifyanomalies→Expert reviewaudit trail→Learncorrection → new rule

What I built

An independent Python reconciliation library compares the pension ERP, the accounting system and technical ledgers transaction by transaction, with exact and time-based matching and accounting invariants. Extraction is automated with Playwright; results and anomaly lists land in SharePoint for review.

How it stays trustworthy

Every anomaly is classified and traceable to its source rows, so reviewers see which control ran and why. Corrections stay with accountable people, and every run can be reproduced. The value is first pedagogy and risk reduction, then capacity: an estimated 3–4 weeks of manual work avoided per foundation.

Self-improving Through the expert: every correction an accountant makes becomes a new rule or test case, so the next run catches it automatically.

02

Reusable Pension ERP Migration Pipeline

Every client migration used to start from scratch.

1 wk → 1 hper migration
2 layersof checks: rules + counter-agent
1 coretested library, reused per client
Profilesource data→Domain packagesversioned · tested→PipelineDagster→Validateend-to-end reconciliation→Counter-agentno self-grading

What I built

A tested pension domain core, packaged as versioned pip-installable libraries and orchestrated in Dagster: profiling, mapping, transformation, validation and SFTP integration. Each new client reuses the core instead of a one-off migration script.

How it stays trustworthy

Deterministic validation and end-to-end reconciliation run first. A multi-agent setup with an adversarial counter-agent then cross-checks the result, so no agent grades its own work.

Rule When models disagree on a business rule, a ticket opens in Linear for its owner, who improves the rule: as deterministic code, or as a judgment guideline for the agent.

03

AI Discovery Co-Coach

Business needs arrive vague, and IT becomes the bottleneck.

~50 h/mofreed on an optimized process
~2 daysto build
Ownersemployees own their process
Employee explainsthe problem→AI challengesassumptions · exceptions→Requirementsacceptance criteria→Prototypelow-risk automation→Independent checkshuman gate

What I built

A co-coach that interviews employees about their own process, challenges assumptions and edge cases, and turns the answers into structured requirements, acceptance criteria and a low-risk prototype. Business teams describe the problem; the agent does the structuring.

How it stays trustworthy

Independent checks and a human gate decide what goes live. Employees stay the owners of their process, and IT stops being the bottleneck for every small automation. Where ownership is unclear, a human arbitrates, not the agent.

Limit Agreement between agents is not independent proof.

The reusable blueprint

Domain core

Business rules live in isolated, versioned and tested packages with explicit contracts. This is the part that must be right, so it stays deterministic.

Agents & interfaces

Python, Dagster, Docker, APIs and agents are replaceable adapters around the core. Models change; the rules and their tests remain.

Evaluation & human gate

Regression tests, evals and reconciliations measure every change. A human approves what ships, and every incident becomes new knowledge.
